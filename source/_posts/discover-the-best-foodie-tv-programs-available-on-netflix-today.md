## Discover the Newest MacBook on the Market Today
 Apple's been on a tear with the latest updates to its MacBook line of computers. The newest MacBooks are the M3-powered 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air.

![The M3 MacBook Air in 13-inch and 15-inch options.](https://www.lifewire.com/thmb/EOD-IRgFrqMGgevnnpW1AvKqKbs=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/Apple-MacBook-Air-2-up-front-240304-4fc0ab043fe04df5b19ee1b2107656cd.jpg)

 The newest MacBook Air machines don't differ drastically from the M2 models, but they're faster and more capable overall—and still offer the ultra-thin build users know and love.

## MacBook Air (M3): 13-inch and 15-inch Models

 The M3 MacBook Air arrived upon the heels of the 14-inch and 16-inch M3, M3 Max, and M3 Pro models. These MacBook Air laptops carry over the strengths from the M2 lineup with a few noticeable upgrades, including support for two external displays and performance that rivals the latest MacBook Pros.

 Here's a look at M3 MacBook Air specs:

* **Chip** : Apple M3, 8-core CPU, 10-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine
* **Memory** : 8GB, up to 24GB of unified memory
* **Storage** : 256GB, up to 2TB SSD
* **Battery** : Up to 18 hours
* **Display** : 13.6- and 15.3-inch Retina display with True Tone technology
* **Ports and charging** : Two Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports

## 14-inch MacBook Pro with M3 Chip

 The M3 chip in the 14-inch MacBook Pro represents the base option in the M3 chip family. Like its higher-performing counterparts, the M3 chips introduces accelerated mesh shading and ray tracing and a faster GPU.

 Here are some key specs for the 14-inch[M3 MacBook Pro](https://www.lifewire.com/m3-macbook-pro-7979159) :

* **Chip** : Apple M3, 8-core CPU, 10-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine
* **Memory** : 8GB, up to 24GB of unified memory
* **Storage** : 512GB, up to 2TB SSD
* **Battery** : Up to 22 hours
* **Display** : 14.2-inch Retina XDR display with True Tone and ProMotion technology
* **Ports and charging** : Two Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports, HDMI, SDXC card slot

## MacBook Pro (M3 Pro): 14-inch and 16-inch Models

 The M3 Pro chip is available in both the 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro. This chip increases processing power, storage, and memory.

 Key M3 Pro MacBook specs include:

* **Chip** : Apple M3 Pro, up to 12-core CPU, up to 18-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine
* **Memory** : 18GB, up to 128GB of unified memory
* **Storage** : 512GB, up to 8TB SSD
* **Battery** : Up to 22 hours
* **Display** : 14.2- and 16.2-inch Retina XDR display with True Tone technology
* **Ports and charging** : Three Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports, HDMI, SDXC card slot

## 13-inch MacBook Pro With M2 Chip

 The[M2 MacBook Pro](https://www.lifewire.com/apples-m2-macbook-pro-isnt-the-successor-it-should-be-5496651) is identical to the 13-inch M1 model in every way except for the chip. It even includes a Touch Bar and a headphone jack. The M2 model can be upgraded with more memory and storage, but aside from faster processing, the M1 and M2 models are the same out of the box.

 If you already have a MacBook with the M1 chip, there's no need to upgrade. That said, the M2 MacBook Pro is a budget-friendly alternative to the more expensive 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro models. But think of it more like the 13-inch M2 MacBook Air rather than the 14- or 16-inch MacBook Pro.  

 Here's an overview of the specs:

* **Chip** : Apple M2, 8-core CPU, 10-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine
* **Memory** : Up to 24GB of unified memory
* **Storage** : Up to 2TB SSD
* **Battery** : Up to 20 hours
* **Display** : 13.3-inch Retina display with True Tone technology
* **Ports and charging** : Two Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports
* **Sensors** : Touch ID and Touch Bar, Force Touch trackpad

 The Apple MacBook has made its presence known since 2006\. The earliest models include the original MacBook and the first versions of the MacBook Pro.

 You can find a comprehensive list of all[MacBook Pro models](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108052) and[MacBook Air](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102869) releases starting from 2006 and 2009, respectively, on Apple's website. Here's a quick overview of MacBook models as they've appeared over the years.

* MacBook Pro 13-inch, M1 (2021)
* MacBook Pro 14-inch and 16-inch, M1 Pro or M1 Max (2021)
* MacBook Pro 13-inch Intel and M1 (2020)
* MacBook Air Retina, 13-inch and M1 (2020)
* MacBook Pro 13-inch, 15-inch, and 16-inch (2019)
* MacBook Air Retina, 13-inch (2018-2019)
* MacBook Air 13-inch (2017)
* MacBook Pro 13-inch and 15-inch (2016-2018)
* MacBook Pro Retina, 13-inch and 15-inch (2012-2015)
