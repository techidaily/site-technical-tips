## Find Your New Favorite: 10 Cost-Free TV Platforms Comparable To Netflix

 You don't need a special streaming device to play free TV shows and movies. These services have mobile apps so you can start a show on your smartphone or tablet and cast the show to a smart TV. These are our 10 favorite free streaming apps that are easy to use, play on a variety of devices, and contain tons of viewing entertainment.

## [ Free and Paid Shows and Movies: Fandango at Home](https://www.vudu.com/content/movies/free) 

![Free documentaries at Fandango at Home](https://www.lifewire.com/thmb/DZQeY2B8vt2S4EeJ6wQPJYAcoWU=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/fandango-at-home-vudu-free-movies-7d0b665536274c308422a88c4361fd91.png) 

 What We Like

* A dedicated Free section that's easy to browse.
* Not very many commercials.
* Create custom playlists.

 What We Don't Like

* Need a free Fandango at Home or Walmart account.
* Some issues casting from mobile device to smart TV.
* Commercials start in awkward places during a show.

 Not everything on the Fandango at Home streaming service is free. Most of the movies and TV shows are available for rent. However, Fandango at Home's free movies include thousands of comedies, indie films, TV classics, anime, kids' shows, and special collections.

 Watch Fandango at Home on your favorite device. Cast from Android, iOS, and Windows 10 devices to a smart TV. Use your Xbox or PlayStation game console. Or play on your Apple TV, Roku, or Chromecast streaming device.

## [ Best for Smart TVs: Xumo](https://www.xumo.com/) 

![Watch Xumo on a smart TV](https://www.lifewire.com/thmb/jcdTC31yPmb0UXEaB-DRYc_G0A0=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/netflix-alternative-03-c735c9d89e1e44babf59630e8995d375.jpg) 

 What We Like

* Mobile apps cast to a smart TV.
* Hundreds of TV and on demand channels.
* Content changes frequently.

 What We Don't Like

* Can't watch shows in a web browser.
* Search finds more TV shows than movies.
* Not easy to pause movies and shows.

 Xumo offers a variety of TV channels that are similar to those found on broadcast TV. The Xumo app opens with a list of over 300 channels of live streaming content. You'll find food and travel shows, comedy channels, lifestyle stations, music shows, and more.

 The list of on-demand movies and TV shows is long. The best way to find a show is to browse the dozens of genres and collections, but the search function could be better, and doesn't return results for very many movies.

 Xumo powers more smart TVs than other programs like Netflix. If you have an LG, Vizio, Sharp, Philips, Magnavox, Panasonic, or Sanyo smart TV, Xumo is available for free. Xumo also works with Roku TV, iOS devices, and Android devices.

[ iOS ](https://apps.apple.com/app/xumo-movies-tv-shows/id1068337978)

## [ Perfect for Streaming Devices: Tubi](https://tubitv.com/) 

![Free TV and movie streaming from Tubi](https://www.lifewire.com/thmb/-iQDrRwkX4u1wQ4-zuj2rwKyWrc=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/netflix-alternative-02-b69e3a7b03c14c4bad824fbc64c88bc9.jpg) 

 What We Like

* Great search function.
* Educational shows for children.
* Mobile apps cast to a smart TV.

 What We Don't Like

* Closed captioning text may be too small for some.
* Need an account to set up shows to binge watch.
* Movies sometimes magically rewind a few seconds.

 Tubi offers thousands of on-demand movies and TV shows. As with most free streaming services, Tubi doesn't offer the latest releases of major box office movies. However, you will find some interesting titles. The search function in Tubi is awesome. Enter a few characters or a random word, and you're presented with a curious list of interesting and unfamiliar titles.

 Tubi is available on 10 streaming devices. You'll find Tubi on the popular Roku, Apple TV, Amazon Fire TV, Xbox, Playstation, and Chromecast devices. It's also available for TiVO, Android TV, Xfinity, and Cox Contour.

 If you have a smart TV from Samsung, Sony, or Vizio, the Tubi app is installed on the TV. There are also mobile and tablet apps for Android and iOS, and you can watch Tubi in a web browser.

**Download for** :

[ iOS ](https://apps.apple.com/app/tubi-tv-stream-free-movies/id886445756)
