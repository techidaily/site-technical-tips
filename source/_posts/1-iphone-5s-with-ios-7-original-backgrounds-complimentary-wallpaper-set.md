Apple officially released iOS 7 on September 18, 2013\. In only 48 hours, more than 40% of Apple devices upgraded to this latest iOS version. In this version, the Chief Designer Jonathan Ive completely overturned Apple's aethestic standard by totally flat design.

No matter you like it or not, the iOS 7 has made a new activitation record is without arguement. And I believe there will be more and more people loving the new design after getting used to it.

An important reason why many people don't like the iOS 7 flat icons and patterns is they are still using a pretty skeuomorphic wallpaper, this kind of conflict won't make users feel beautiful.

So here we collect all the default WallPapers in iOS 7, they are all suit for iPhone 5c and iPhone 5s.
